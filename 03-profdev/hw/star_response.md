# STAR response

**Question:** Tell me about a time you faced a challenge while working on a project or with a team. What did you do, and what was the result?

## Situation

I was working on a group project in Financial Accounting and Reporting that was covering Pfizer vs Moderna's financials and we had individually given each person a task to complete by a certain date, and then by then we would compile the work and cross check it for validity and understanding. As the date approached, a team member let us know very late that he would not be able to complete his part of the task.

## Task

We needed to get the missing team members section covered and submitted before the deadline without sacrificing the quality of the work.

## Action

I had to take hold of the situation, particularly because we were running low on time. I analyzed the part he had been given and distributed work to each of the other members so that it could be completed efficently and accurately. While we were on a tight schedule, I wanted to do my best to not sacrifice the quality of the work, and I achieved this by using what we had at our disposal and creating a collaborative enviroment with the remaining team members.

## Result

We were able to successfully submit the final project on time with minimal sacrifice in quality, thanks to well organized team collaboration, efficent work, and great use of our resources.
